## Badge policy

Badges are assigned per story, not per page: a story that is only an
attributed claim, a "reportedly", or an aggregator listing gets CLAIM
even when the underlying event is real; a projection or an expected
event gets FORECAST. Nothing unverified is ever marked REPORTED.
